L6 · Composition Plan

This composition plan documents the layout, positioning, layering, and shape enhancements planned for Postmarked: The Lost Art of Sending Postcards. The design combines vintage postcard details with a modern editorial magazine layout.

Element Content purpose Normal-flow baseline Chosen technique and why Containing block / stacking context Fallback or disengagement Required test
Vintage postcard Photo Illustrates the history and personal appeal of postcards. Image appears as a regular figure with a caption Border-radius softens corners and clip-path creates an angled paper like edges. The feature figure provides the image's visual frame. Without clip-path, the image remains visible in its normal rectangular shape. Disable clip-path and confirm the image remains visible and readable.
Oval botanical illustration Adds a romantic vintage illustration beside the article text. Image appears before the section paragraphs. Float and shape-outside create curved editorial text wrap. The article section contains the float using flow-root. Without shape-outside, text wraps around the rectangular floating image box on desktop. On mobile, the image returns to normal flow. Disable shape-outside and confirm text remains readable.
Layering System Controls which decorative and navigational elements appear above others. Elements follow their normal document order. CSS custom properties define consistent z-index levels. Positioned elements and the isolated collage control stacking. Content remains readable when decorative overlap is removed. Inspect stacking and confirm no important content is hidden.
Header issue badge Displays the decorative "Wish You Were Here". The badge follows the heading and introduction in normal flow. Absolute positioning anchors the badge to the feature header on desktop. The positioned feature header is the containing block. On smaller screens, the badge returns to normal layout positioning. Check badge placement at desktop and mobile widths.
In This Story navigation Provides quick links to the three article sections. The navigation appears before the article in reading order. Sticky positioning keeps navigation available when reading. The story-layout container bounds sticky movement before documentation. On narrow screens, navigation uses normal positioning. Scrolling through the article and confirm navigation does not overlap documentation.
Overlapping postcard collage Creates a vintage scrapbook-style introduction. Two illustrated postcards appear side by side. CSS Grid, transform, negative margins, and z-index create controlled overlap. The isolated postcard-collage creates a local stacking context. On smaller screens, the postcards stack without overlap. Check overlap and text visibility at desktop, tablet, and mobile widths.
